Fashion retail sourcing, buying & brand development

About

Scott MacRae is a fashion retail professional based in the United Kingdom, currently working as a consultant with Brands In Ltd. His career spans product sourcing, buying, and business strategy across the apparel industry, with hands-on experience in international sourcing markets including Bangladesh.

Scott began his career as a womenswear assistant buyer before progressing to senior buying roles in menswear and branded fashion. He has since advised retailers and brands on sourcing strategy, range development, and supplier relationships, combining a deep passion for product and textiles with a commercial focus on results.

He holds a BA in Visual Arts and Culture from the University of Salford.

Key achievements

  • Built a 15+ year buying career at Next Sourcing Ltd, progressing from assistant buyer to branded fashion buyer.
  • Established branded fashion ranges for the Next Directory business, onboarding new brands into a leading multi-channel retailer.
  • Sourced and developed menswear denim and casual trouser ranges for a national high-street retailer.
  • Worked with international liaison offices and supply bases, including Bangladesh, developing product from concept to delivery.
  • Held buying and product roles at George at Asda and the Dishang Group, one of the world's largest apparel manufacturers.
  • Founded Macrae Consulting Services Ltd, advising fashion businesses on sourcing, buying strategy, and brand development.
