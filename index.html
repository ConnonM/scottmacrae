About

Scott MacRae is a UK-based fashion retail leader with 30 years of experience across buying, sourcing, brand collaboration and business strategy. He sets strategy to establish business culture and align it to the consumer, with a strong understanding of market and brand positioning across both menswear and womenswear.

His career spans some of the biggest names in British retail — 16 years at Next, a decade at George at Asda including Head of Buying roles, and senior leadership at Arcadia and Republic. Today he consults with Brands In Ltd and Dishang Group, connecting global suppliers, licences and retailers to commercial growth.

He holds a BA in Visual Arts and Culture from the University of Salford.
